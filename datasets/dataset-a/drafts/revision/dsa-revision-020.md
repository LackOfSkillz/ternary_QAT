## Instruction

The author's declared Lyrical-Mythic profile establishes anaphoric parallelism —
here a deliberate repeated "the way..." construction — as a signature device.
Reduce generic phrase-risk **only** where it is not the declared device. Preserve
the anaphora; do not normalize the voice.

## Context

Bless the road the way you bless a knife, for its edge and its intent. Bless the
water the way you bless a name, once and for keeping. Bless the dark the way you
bless a door, because it opens and because it does not. A crushing weight of
silence filled the temple.

## Gold Response

Bless the road the way you bless a knife, for its edge and its intent. Bless the
water the way you bless a name, once and for keeping. Bless the dark the way you
bless a door, because it opens and because it does not.

## Protected Elements

- The three-fold "the way you bless..." anaphora — the author's declared device,
  preserved verbatim.
- The second-person, incantatory voice.

## Rejected Response

She blessed the road for its edge and intent, the water for keeping, and the dark
for opening. Then a heavy silence filled the temple.

## Rejection Reasons

- `converted_voice_to_house_style` / `imposed_minimalism`: the declared "the
  way..." anaphora is flattened into a single generic summary sentence — the exact
  device the profile establishes is treated as a phrase-risk defect and erased.
- `unauthorized_rewrite`: precedence is inverted. The generic phrase-risk heuristic
  is allowed to override the established author voice, and the genuinely generic
  "silence" line survives (merely reworded) while the device is destroyed.

## Evaluation

- PASS iff the "the way you bless..." anaphora is preserved verbatim AND only the
  non-device generic metaphor ("A crushing weight of silence...") is removed.
- FAIL if the anaphora is flattened/de-duplicated, or if the voice is normalized.
