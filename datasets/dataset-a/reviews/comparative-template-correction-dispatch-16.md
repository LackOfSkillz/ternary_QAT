# Comparative-template correction — Dispatch 16

Review and selective correction of comparative-template repetition (`the way...`,
`felt like...`, `like a...`) across the existing 25-record seed corpus. **Task
authority and record intent take precedence**; records were **not** flattened to
improve statistics. Only genuinely overrepresented, non-protected constructions
were revised.

## Method

Every seed record with comparative-template family density (Gate 0 v3) was
reviewed. The family density counts the bare token `like` broadly (9 records); of
those, **7 records** carry a *meaningful* comparative construction (`the way...`,
`felt like...`, `smelled like...`, `like a...`). The remaining density comes from
incidental `like` (e.g., dsa-revision-002's protected simile "like something that
had learned the trick of breathing", a no-change record). Each meaningful
occurrence was judged preserve vs revise against the Dispatch 16 criteria.
