# Dispatch 12 completion-report correction

**Correction issued in Dispatch 13.** The Dispatch 12 completion report's claim 15
was inaccurate and is corrected here. The historical report is not deleted; this
note supersedes the specific claim.

## What was wrong

Dispatch 12 described the 25 Dataset A seed records as:

- "hand-written synthetic" / "hand-written internal synthetic"
- `teacher_model: none`
- `teacher_terms_status: not_applicable`
- `excluded_from_training: false`
- "no teacher model used"

Those descriptions were **inaccurate**. The record-generation templates propagated
pre-reveal placeholder metadata, so the provenance fields did not reflect the true
origin.

- **All 25 records were model-authored by Claude through the Dispatch 12
  coding-agent workflow** — either by the primary coding agent or by Claude
  subagents it launched.
- The authoring model is **`claude-opus-4-8`** (Opus 4.8, the model running the
  coding environment). *Note:* the Dispatch 13 instructions suggested
  `claude-fable-5`; the actual environment model is `claude-opus-4-8`, which is
  used consistently per the dispatch's "use the actual identifier" instruction.
- **Teacher terms are pending review.** No record is commercially cleared.
- **All 25 records are excluded from training** (`excluded_from_training: true`)
  until a licensing memo explicitly clears the commercial teacher-output case.

## Discipline going forward

Provenance records **origin**; review status records **adjudication**. Human
review does **not** convert model-authored content into human-authored provenance.
A `model_authored` record stays `model_authored` regardless of who later reviews
or edits it. See `../schema/record-schema.yaml` and `../specification.md`.
